The People Behind The Stories

MEET
THE TEAM

MEET
CHANDAN KUMAR
Founder & Director

Chandan Kumar is the Founder & Director of Story Telling Disease, a content creation company. With a diverse background in theatre, film, and media, he brings extensive experience to his work. His credits include fiction writing for Colors Kannada, copywriting for Radio City FM, and directing numerous successful ads and short films. His recent Kannada short film "Nam Kaaladalli Hing Irlilla" has garnered recognition at multiple film festivals.

LOKESH LINGARAJU
Technical Head

Lokesh, a St. Joseph's University film graduate, is a versatile cinematographer and art director. He has contributed his expertise to numerous advertisements, films, short films, and web series, demonstrating a keen eye for compelling visual storytelling. His technical mastery and artistic vision form the visual backbone of every STD production.
