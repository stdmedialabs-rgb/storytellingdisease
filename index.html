Established in 2020, Story Telling Disease (STD) is a premier advertising agency based in Bengaluru, specialising in Ad films, corporate videos, and movie promotions.

With a strong creative vision and a results-driven approach, we have successfully collaborated with leading brands and businesses across India — from jewellery to tech, real estate to entertainment.

We are infected with the incurable urge to tell stories. Our team of exceptional writers, directors, cinematographers, and editors forge genuine connections between your brand and your audience.

How we work

From concept to final cut, we create powerful ad films that tell your brand's story with emotional depth and cinematic craft. Each film is built to leave a lasting impression.

We produce high-quality corporate videos — from internal communications and training films to event coverage and brand documentaries that reflect your company culture.

Trailers, teasers, promotional content and social campaigns that build hype and drive audiences to cinemas. We understand the film industry inside out.

Visually compelling music videos and full live event coverage — from multi-camera setups to creative direction and post-production.

Short-form reels, brand films, product videos and ongoing social content designed for engagement and reach across Instagram, YouTube and beyond.
